- The event triggers:
  - The coordinator pushes on a committed send, on each committed Broadcast copy, on Register (that session's pending mail) and on hello (that host's pending mail).
  - The host daemon pushes on a committed deliver, and from its inbox on a local Register. It forwards an ack upstream, and replays its receipts on hello.
  - Claim expiry is read from the committed deadline at each request (`Claims::live`).
  - Unregister happens in three ways: explicitly (the adapter on stdin EOF, then the daemon `release`, then the coordinator commit); through a disconnect (the daemon's connection-close report, or the coordinator's `HostLink` drop); and through `expire()` at a Broadcast, claim, relabel or Board read.

How the check works:

- It runs the release binaries on loopback: a coordinator with `--unregister-after-ms 2000` and host daemons a, b and c.
- It has seven phases: directed, broadcast, register, hello, claims, leave and offline. In each phase, one trigger is the only path to the result.
- Its idle windows fail if any core log grows while nothing sends a request. That catches any sweep that runs.
- Every daemon that is alive when the coordinator returns is a fresh process, so the reconnect backoff never produces a result.
- At the end it checks exactly-once: one mail row and one receipt per id, and each body once per Store.
